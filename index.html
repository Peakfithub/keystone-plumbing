Common questions

Before you call

Are your estimates really free?

Yes. Quotes from this form are free. For on-site diagnostics there's a flat $89 fee, which we waive if you book the repair with us.

Do you pull permits?

We pull permits for water heater replacements, repipes, sewer work, gas lines and any job that requires one under local code, and we schedule the inspection.

Can you work after hours for my business?

Yes. Restaurants and retail clients often book overnight or early-morning windows so the work never interrupts service.

What should I do during a leak?

Shut off the main water valve (usually near the water meter or where the line enters the building), turn off the water heater, then call our emergency line.

Do you offer financing?

Financing is available on jobs over $1,500, including water heaters, repipes and sewer line replacements.
